# Ethics and Privacy

Data collection was limited to the author's own posts and read-only, creator-visible analytics that LinkedIn exposes to the account owner. No viewer identities, private messages, or authentication data were collected. No third-party content is reproduced. Race-related labels apply to post topics, not to any person.

This is independent research and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or conducted on behalf of LinkedIn or any employer.

Contributors and issue reporters must never upload private LinkedIn data, raw exports, cookies, tokens, or authentication material.
