# Limitations

- Single-account observational design; not platform-wide.
- Cumulative impressions measured at unequal post ages.
- Detailed analytics pilot is purposefully selected, not representative.
- Most classification text may be truncated.
- Race-related labels are preliminary automated labels; 0 of 459 candidates are human-approved at the frozen checkpoint.
- Frozen race intervals use HC3 errors and omit detected serial dependence (lag-1 residual autocorrelation around 0.29).
- Mixture components are descriptive regimes, not confirmed production stages.
- Absence of moderation notices is not evidence of fair treatment.
- Nonsignificance is uncertainty, not proof of equal treatment.
