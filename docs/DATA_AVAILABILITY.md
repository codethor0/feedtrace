# Data Availability

## Publicly available

- Sanitized non-race analytic table (`data/sanitized/`)
- Aggregate tables (`data/aggregate/`)
- Frozen predictive baselines (`results/model_outputs/`)
- Rendered figures (`figures/rendered/`)
- Paper PDFs and Markdown sources (`paper/`)

## Withheld for privacy

- Raw browser exports and interim captures
- Full post text and preview text
- Direct LinkedIn URLs and activity URNs
- Viewer identities and private messages
- Cookies, tokens, credentials, and authentication material
- Race-label candidate records and review queues
- Private reviewer packets and collection logs

## Boundary rule

Privacy protection takes priority over maximal reproducibility. Analyses that require withheld inputs are reported in the paper but are not represented as independently executable from this repository.
