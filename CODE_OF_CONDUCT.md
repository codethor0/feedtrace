# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the research record

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Uploading private LinkedIn data or credentials
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the private security contact process described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or ban contributors who violate this code.
